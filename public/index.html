How to read these results +

This is a learning experiment, not a demonstrated trading edge. The first version uses DEX Screener aggregate quotes about once a minute. It does not ingest every on-chain trade, detect the first seconds of a launch, or replay historical trades.

“5m rise” is the provider’s past five-minute price change. Liquidity, volume, and pair age are filters, not a safety audit. Holder concentration, mint permissions, bundled wallets, and manipulation are not checked.

Simulated fills include configurable fees and slippage plus a simple liquidity-impact estimate. Real fills, priority fees, failed transactions, and MEV can be worse. Exit triggers are sampled, so an exit can happen later or at a worse price than the target. Missing liquidity blocks exits; unavailable liquidity is valued at zero in paper equity. Stale marks can distort results.

The latency metric measures a data request from Cloudflare, not blockchain confirmation speed or the age of the provider’s source data. Results start accumulating here; no historical profit record has been fabricated. Compare rules on fresh future observations before drawing conclusions.

Coinbase is not connected. No private keys are stored and this app cannot place real orders.
