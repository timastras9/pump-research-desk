Import format
{"source":"provider / capture date","mint":"token mint","trades":[{"id":"signature:instruction","time":1780000000000,"priceUsd":0.0001,"volumeUsd":5}]}

The importer requires at least two observations. Use a distinct instruction ID when a transaction contains multiple trades. Record the provider and SOL/USD conversion method in source. Candles use the supplied timestamp; reception timestamps do not establish on-chain timing.

What counts as progress?

Choose rules before collecting a fresh sample. Compare the same rules on later observations, include losing and untradeable markets, and inspect results with higher costs. A selected winning token or a synthetic demo is not evidence of an edge.

Signals use two completed 5-second candles, rising volume, one position at a time and a 30-second re-entry cooldown. Orders fill only at later observed trades after the configured delay. Gaps can leave exits unfilled; the replay never invents a final sale. Remaining positions are marked estimates.
